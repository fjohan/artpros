Rule
min adjacent angle
For each adjacent pair, compute the angle where those two triangles would just touch: theta_i = atan((center[i+1] - center[i]) / (mag[i] + mag[i+1])). The pulse train uses theta = min(theta_i), so the most crowded pair touches and the other pairs remain separated.
